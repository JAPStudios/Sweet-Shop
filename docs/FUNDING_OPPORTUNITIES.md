# Funding routes for Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry

Research date: 2026-11-26

Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is an open online marketplace intended for individuals and organisations to sell their own creations independently without listing fees or arbitrary restrictions. It is also intended to provide lower-cost or free access to life essentials based on income recognised by Butter Bank, another public service. The marketplace brief specifies product verification before publication, seller-union membership, JaMail organisation accounts and intended pickup/delivery through UnterSous. These are separate services with distinct stated roles; this description does not claim that their technical integrations are already operational.

The stated relationship is that HeLLP owns Sweet Shop: Survival Service, and Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is under Sweet Shop: Survival Service. Those names and relationships must not be collapsed into one name or treated as proof that any particular name is the registered legal applicant. Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is the project being funded. The grant applicant must be identified from the relevant registration and organisational records, using the exact legal name and evidence required by the funding programme.

## Best current grant lead: USDA Agriculture Innovation Center Program

Official programme: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/agriculture-innovation-center-program

- Open application period: September 9, 2026 through January 31, 2027.
- Programme funding: $2.5 million; maximum grant: $1 million; three awards are expected.
- Nonprofit organisations are among the eligible applicants.
- The applicant must operate an Agriculture Innovation Center that provides services to agricultural producers developing and marketing value-added agricultural products.
- Eligible services include producer business development, market development, organisational and financial advice, process and systems development, product development, packaging/label evaluation and value-chain coordination.
- Matching funds must be at least one-third of the total project budget. Cash or eligible third-party in-kind contributions may count; contributions must be available during the project period and used for allowable expenses.
- Application requires the registrations and forms specified by USDA, including SAM.gov registration and the programme's required application forms. Confirm the current checklist with the USDA programme contact before submission.

Fit: this could support a defined Digital Pantry programme serving agricultural producers who sell value-added food products through the marketplace. It does not fund the whole general-purpose marketplace, all product categories, general retail operations or ordinary seller listings. It is not a fit unless the proposed work genuinely operates the required producer-service centre and can document the one-third match.

Official opportunity listing: https://grants.gov/search-results-detail/363833

## Conditional technology-development route: EDA FY2027 Build to Scale

Official programme: https://www.eda.gov/funding/funding-opportunities/fiscal-year-2027-build-scale

- Open; deadline February 26, 2027 at 4:59 p.m. Eastern.
- Emerging Path: up to $1 million over 36 months. Growth Path: up to $7 million over 48 months.
- The programme supports regional innovation and technology-commercialisation ecosystems, with focus on specified critical technology areas including machine learning, semiconductors, quantum, robotics, biotechnology, cybersecurity, advanced energy and advanced materials.
- A nonprofit applicant must have support from a state or political subdivision. EDA's current FAQ describes evidence such as a resolution or letter signed by an appropriate state or local official or legislator.
- A nonprofit does not have to be a 501(c)(3), but must provide evidence of nonprofit status, including at minimum articles of incorporation, a certificate of good standing and bylaws.
- A 50% matching share is mandatory: each federal dollar must be matched by at least one dollar. Matching contributions must be committed, available, unencumbered and unrestricted at the required time. Eligible documented in-kind contributions may count; the match cannot be waived.
- An active SAM.gov registration is required before submission. Applications are submitted through EDA's EDGE system.

Fit: not a general grant for building or operating an online marketplace. It is only worth pursuing if the proposed work independently meets a listed critical-technology area and is delivered as a regional commercialisation programme with the required public-sector support and match. A general retail marketplace alone does not establish eligibility.

Official FAQ: https://www.eda.gov/funding/programs/build-to-scale/2027/frequently-asked-questions

## Future-cycle possibility: USDA Rural Business Development Grants

Official New York programme page: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/rural-business-development-grants/ny

- The FY2026 application window closed June 30, 2026.
- Eligible applicants include public bodies, federally recognised Tribes and nonprofit entities primarily serving rural areas.
- There is no programme-wide cost-share requirement.
- Eligible activities include technology-based economic development, feasibility studies, entrepreneur training, rural business incubators and long-term strategic planning.
- Funds must benefit eligible rural areas; the programme evaluates local job creation, economic need and local economic-development priorities.

Fit: a future application could focus on rural small-seller access, seller training or a rural business-support component connected to Digital Pantry. It is not currently open and should not be presented as money available now. Ask the New York USDA Rural Development office for the next application schedule before preparing an application.

## Future-cycle possibility: USDA Rural Cooperative Development Grant

Official New York programme page: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/rural-cooperative-development-grant-program/ny

- The listed application window is closed; the last stated deadline was September 15, 2025.
- The programme funds nonprofit institutions that operate cooperative-development centres and provide technical assistance to start, expand or improve rural cooperatives and mutually owned businesses.
- The USDA fact sheet states a maximum award of $200,000 and a 25% match requirement.

Fit: potentially relevant to a genuine rural seller-cooperative development programme. It is not a direct grant for the marketplace software or a substitute for deciding the seller union's actual legal and operating arrangements.

## SNAP online retailer authorisation: relevant to eligible food sales, not a development grant

Official online retailer requirements: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/retailer/online/requirements

- SNAP retailer authorisation is free, but it is not a source of development funding.
- A retailer must first be eligible and authorised to accept SNAP. Additional Food and Nutrition Administration authorisation is required for online purchasing.
- An online-only retailer must submit a letter of intent to SNAPOnline@usda.gov and follow the separate internet-retailer application process.
- The online storefront must support secure EBT PIN entry through a technically approved third-party processor, eligible-food restrictions, refunds, split tender and other required controls.
- End-to-end production testing with FNA and the relevant state agency is required before launch.
- SNAP applies only to eligible food purchases, not every category of life essentials or general marketplace goods.

This is a later operational pathway for eligible food sales. It should not be represented as a simple universal retailer registration or as funding for the marketplace.

## 501(c)(6) classification: do not assume it fits the marketplace activity

IRS guidance: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/other-non-profits/business-leagues

A 501(c)(6) business league is generally an association organised to promote the common business interests of a line of business. Its activities must improve business conditions rather than primarily provide particular services to individual persons. The IRS states that an organisation primarily engaging in a regular business ordinarily carried on for profit does not qualify, even if the activity is cooperative or only self-sustaining.

The seller-union concept may warrant separate analysis as a possible association activity. Operating the marketplace itself as a regular retail or marketplace business raises a distinct classification question. The 501(c)(6) label must not be assumed to authorise or exempt the marketplace's operating activity. The actual applicant's purposes, governance, member support, revenue model and activities must be assessed before choosing a tax classification.

## Technology credits, not cash

### AWS Nonprofit Credit Program

Official programme: https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/nonprofit-credit-program/

AWS lists 501(c)(1), 501(c)(3), 501(c)(8), 501(c)(9), 501(c)(11), 501(c)(12), 501(c)(14) and 501(c)(15) organisations as eligible categories. A 501(c)(6) designation is not on that list. For US organisations, AWS directs applicants through TechSoup and its partner NGOs. This is AWS promotional credit, not cash or unrestricted project funding. Eligibility depends on the actual applicant's approved designation.

### GitHub for Nonprofits

Official programme: https://github.com/solutions/industry/nonprofits

GitHub's current programme is limited to verified 501(c)(3) or equivalent organisations that also meet its non-governmental, non-academic, non-commercial and non-political requirements. It provides a free GitHub Team plan or a discounted Enterprise plan, not a cash grant. Do not assume eligibility for a different nonprofit classification.

## Programmes not to treat as matches

- NY BRICKS Round 2 is open until December 7, 2026, but funds community-centre construction, renovation and modernisation. It is not a grant for marketplace software or online retail operations.
- USDA Rural Business Development Grants are closed for the 2026 cycle.
- USDA Rural Cooperative Development Grants are closed.
- The EDA Build to Scale programme is not unrestricted operating support and has a mandatory 50% matching share.
- The Agriculture Innovation Center programme is limited to producer services for value-added agricultural products and has a mandatory one-third match.

## Practical application sequence

1. Use the stated relationships accurately: HeLLP owns Sweet Shop: Survival Service; Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is under Sweet Shop: Survival Service. Separately verify which registered legal applicant will submit and administer a particular grant. Do not infer that identity from the project names or their stated relationship.
2. For the Agriculture Innovation Center opportunity, define only the agricultural-producer services that Digital Pantry would actually provide, document the producers and eligible service area, and establish the required one-third match before submitting.
3. In parallel, contact New York USDA Rural Development about the next Rural Business Development Grant cycle and whether a rural seller-support programme would qualify.
4. Pursue EDA Build to Scale only if a specific critical-technology scope, public-sector support and committed 50% matching share can be evidenced.
5. Treat SNAP as a separate retailer-authorisation process for eligible food purchases, not as a funding source.
6. Assess the seller-union purpose separately from marketplace operations before relying on 501(c)(6) status.
7. Treat AWS and GitHub benefits as reductions in infrastructure/development costs, not as cash funding.

No currently open programme identified in this review directly funds the entire general-purpose Digital Pantry marketplace, its general operating costs or all product categories without material restrictions.
