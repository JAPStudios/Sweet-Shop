# Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry

Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is an open online marketplace intended to let individuals and organisations list and sell their own creations independently, without listing fees or arbitrary restrictions. It is also intended to make everyday essentials available at lower prices, including free access where appropriate, based on income recognised by Butter Bank, another public service.

## Marketplace operation

- Everyone who uses Digital Pantry is part of the seller union.
- Sellers access a profile dashboard using their JaMail account and submit product listings.
- Submitted listings remain private until the product has been tested and verified as legitimate.
- Sellers package the requested items. UnterSous pickup and delivery charges are calculated at checkout and added to the order total.
- When an order includes products from multiple sellers or locations, the delivery driver collects all packages before delivering them. Orders from one location receive a consolidated-delivery discount.
- The visual direction is pastel sweets with an Easter-holiday theme.
- Product inventory is intended to include imports from the other JAPStudios stores, affordable essentials and reasonably priced non-essential goods.
- A planned programme will offer handcrafted, made-to-order products produced by incarcerated workers, with financial compensation for their labour.

## Stated service relationships

Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is under Sweet Shop: Survival Service. HeLLP owns Sweet Shop: Survival Service. These names and relationships are stated separately and should not be collapsed into one name.

Butter Bank recognises income for the income-adjusted access described above. JaMail provides individual accounts and organisation access. UnterSous provides pickup and delivery. These are distinct public services.

This repository does not claim that the services' technical integrations are already operational. It also does not specify the legal registration, tax classification or grant-applicant identity of any named project or service.

## Current repository state

At this revision, the repository contains the project overview and initial funding/upstream-source research. It does not yet contain a functioning production marketplace, live seller onboarding, product-verification workflow, checkout, Butter Bank income-recognition integration, JaMail authentication integration or UnterSous dispatch integration.
