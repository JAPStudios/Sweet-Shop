# Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry

Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry is an open online marketplace intended to let individuals and organisations list and sell their own creations without listing fees or arbitrary restrictions. It is also intended to make everyday essentials available at lower prices, including income-adjusted access to essentials through a financial institution.

## Marketplace operation

- Sellers access a profile dashboard using their JaMail account and submit product listings.
- Submitted listings remain private until the product has been tested and verified as legitimate.
- The seller community is the seller union.
- Sellers package the requested items. UnterSous delivery charges are calculated at checkout and added to the order total.
- When an order includes products from multiple sellers, the delivery driver collects the packages before delivering them to the buyer. Orders from one seller/location receive the corresponding consolidated-delivery discount.
- The visual direction is pastel sweets with an Easter-holiday theme.
- Product inventory is intended to include imports from the other JAPStudios stores, affordable essentials and reasonably priced non-essential goods.
- A planned programme will offer handcrafted, made-to-order products produced by incarcerated workers, with financial compensation for their labour.

## Service relationships

The stated operating structure places Sweet Shop: Digital Pantry under Sweet Shop: Survival Service, within the wider HeLLP organisation. Butter Bank provides transaction, billing and credit services. JaMail provides individual accounts and organisation access. UnterSous provides pickup and delivery. Income-adjusted access to essentials is described as operating through a financial institution; this repository does not assign that role to Butter Bank.

These services remain distinct; this repository does not claim that their integrations are already operational.

## Current repository state

At this revision, the repository contains the project overview and initial funding/upstream-source research. It does not yet contain a functioning production marketplace, live seller onboarding, product-verification workflow, checkout, Butter Bank transaction integration, JaMail authentication integration or UnterSous dispatch integration.
